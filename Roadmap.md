# ZEngine roadmap

ZEngine 1.0.0 is released. The current public roadmap is maintained in the
[documentation website](https://jeanphilippekernel.github.io/zodiac-engine-docs/project/roadmap/)
and its source page is [docs/project/roadmap.md](docs/project/roadmap.md).

The present focus is persistent scene authoring, editor interaction, rendering
hardening, asset-pipeline reliability, and reproducible build and distribution
work. Planned systems are design records, not product commitments.
